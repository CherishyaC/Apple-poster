Think Different, 1997

The ad that did not show a product.

By 1997 the brand was weak. The campaign that turned it around launched on 28 September, put black-and-white portraits of well-known figures on television, magazines and billboards, and never once put a product on screen.

It sold a way of seeing the world first. The products followed, and so did the value.
